# Landing page (/)

Scope: `src/components/landing`, rendered at `/` for visitors who are not logged in. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a couple early in planning, in Denmark or marrying abroad (weighted equally). They should understand that Juno does the legwork and they keep the decisions, and start for free. Proof on hand: code-drawn miniatures of the real app and seven photographs; no testimonials or numbers.

Constraints: Danish. Only what works today (see PRODUCT.md). Price stated: free, plus 499 kr once for the designed website and invitation. Juno is cute and animated, but her motion must not distract, and the page must not read as an "AI product". She is a reusable component (`src/components/juno/Juno.tsx`); the app adopts her next.

Extension of the incumbent landing world (laurel, pistachio, champagne cream; Cormorant and Inter), restructured around a new character.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Juno reporting to the couple: a coordinator's status that waits for their yes. It refuses the wedding-photo hero over a feature grid, and the glowing orb that says "AI".

OWN-WORLD: Laurel green fields open and close the page; champagne cream carries the middle, with one pistachio field for the terms. Juno is the only warm thing on it: a little peach cupid with ivory wings and a gold bow, no outline. Her work arrives on cream paper cards with hairline edges. The top bar is a cream pill from the first frame. Cormorant for display, Inter for text.

STORY: The visitor sees what Juno has done, presses the one button on her note, and watches her arrow carry it off. They believe "she does, we decide" because they just did it, and start free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Laurel edge to edge under the cream bar. Left: "Mød Juno, jeres bryllupskoordinator." at display scale, one paragraph, the button "Kom i gang gratis", the price line under it. Right: a stack of cream notes; Juno flies in on a curve and hovers over the top one, her shadow on it, eyes on the note, glancing at the pointer.

FORM: Pinned by the brief (Juno as centrepiece, a coordinator, a cupid); no roll, no seed key. Sections follow a wedding's own order: find, write, compare, then everything from yes to thank-you. Signature interaction: approving her note. Motion grammar: she hovers; in the three steps there is one Juno, who flies to the step being read; nothing else on the page loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
